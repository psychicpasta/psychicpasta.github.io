## Category One : Software Design and Engineering
### Artifact : 3D Replica Scene - CS 330 Computer Graphics and Visualization
### [Original Artifact](https://github.com/psychicpasta/psychicpasta.github.io/tree/main/Category1OriginalArtifact)
### [Enhanced Artifact](https://github.com/psychicpasta/psychicpasta.github.io/tree/main/Category1EnhancedArtifact)

>The artifact that I have selected for this category is a 3D scene that I developed in 07/2025 for the CS 330 course at SNHU. The purpose of the project was to capture a scene of objects in real life, and attempt to recreate that same scene in a digital, 3D environment. This consisted of generating and placing the shapes in a way to properly represent the real life objects, and apply color, texture, and lighting to the scene to make it as realistic as possible.
>I selected this artifact to be included in my ePortfolio because I think it is a unique project that allows me to showcase a fine tuned set of skills. Being able to create 3D art shows a level of mathematical skills that are needed to understand the geometry of the real world. It also promotes my ability to program in C++, a highly respected and desired programming language in the job market. With my enhancement, the user can now turn off (‘N’ key) and turn on (‘M’ key) the lights of the scene at the click of a button on their keyboard. This gives the user more control over the scene, along with the camera movement and projection view changes. I think that this enhancement promotes my abilities on both a design and technical level. On a technical level, it shows that I can connect external input from a device to a program to have major control, which would be useful in a game or other 3D development project. On a design level, it shows that I have a user-focused mindset, and consider giving them more features to interact with as an enhancement to the overall project.
>With these enhancements, I believe that I have met the course outcomes I was striving to meet in Module One. I think the enhanced 3D scene is a technically sound visual communication that accurately represents the real world scene it was intended to replicate. I also was able to put my innovative skills and computing technique to good work to further develop the 3D scene and deliver an industry standard product.
>When I was working on the enhancements, I was immediately reminded how much I enjoyed developing the original artifact in my CS 330 class. It was a fun experience because it was a type of project I have never worked on before, and it was interesting to learn how 3D scenes are developed. As I was working on the enhancement, I actually realized that the original lighting in the artifact could use a touch up. So before I continued adding the M and N button functionality for turning the lights on and off, I tweaked the positioning and strength of the lighting to make it more visually appealing. This was a learning moment for me that code can always be improved and enhanced, no matter what level you think it is currently at. Which was a challenge for me as well because I kept thinking of new ideas to enhance it outside of my proposal. I may even continue working on it after this course is complete.
